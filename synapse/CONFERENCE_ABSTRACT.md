# Conference Abstract — SyNAPSE

## Title

**SyNAPSE: A Privacy-Preserving, Emotion-Aware AAC Platform with Periodic Monitoring and Role-Based Alerts for Students of Determination in UAE Schools**

---

## Abstract (≈250 words)

Children of Determination who are minimally verbal often depend on static augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) boards that do not reflect fluctuating emotional states, while educators and caregivers may miss early signs of distress during classroom or home routines. We present **SyNAPSE** (Synaptic Neurodiverse Augmentative Platform for Speech & Expression), a web-based, local-first system that integrates real-time facial emotion recognition, bilingual (English–Arabic) AAC card generation, and **periodic background monitoring every six minutes** to support timely intervention aligned with UAE inclusive education practice.

SyNAPSE captures webcam frames locally, classifies seven emotions using DeepFace, and—when **cautious affect** (sad, angry, fear, disgust) exceeds a confidence threshold—immediately notifies the **homeroom teacher** during school sessions or the **assigned caregiver** during home sessions via WebSocket and browser notifications. Stakeholders can then guide the child toward adaptive AAC boards generated by a local large language model (Ollama/Llama 3) or rule-based fallbacks, preserving communication opportunity at the moment of need. Session data (emotion logs, card selections, duration) are stored as metadata only; no video is retained, supporting child data protection expectations in KHDA-aware SEND workflows.

The platform serves four roles—student, teacher, SEND officer, and caregiver—and produces Communication Matrix–style PDF reports for monitoring and review. We describe the architecture (FastAPI, React, SQLite, WebSockets), the Chrome extension for sustained monitoring, and deployment options for school LAN or cloud hosting. SyNAPSE demonstrates how low-cost, on-device AI can link affective computing to AAC practice in Gulf inclusive education contexts, advancing SDGs 3 (well-being), 4 (inclusive education), and 10 (reduced inequalities).

**Keywords:** AAC, autism, emotion recognition, inclusive education, KHDA, UAE, DeepFace, WebSocket alerts, privacy-by-design

---

## Short abstract (≈120 words, for poster)

SyNAPSE is an emotion-aware AAC platform for minimally verbal Students of Determination in UAE private schools. Every **six minutes**, a background monitor analyses facial affect locally (DeepFace); cautious emotions trigger **instant alerts** to teachers at school or caregivers at home. Children communicate via bilingual AAC cards adapted to mood and topic. The stack is local-first (FastAPI, React, Ollama, SQLite) with no video storage. Four roles—student, teacher, SEND officer, caregiver—share session evidence and KHDA-aligned PDF reports. A Chrome extension sustains monitoring while the student session is open. SyNAPSE links affective computing to AAC and SEND accountability without cloud API dependency.

## One-line contribution

**Periodic, privacy-preserving emotion monitoring with environment-aware (school/home) alerts that connect detected distress to immediate AAC communication support.**
